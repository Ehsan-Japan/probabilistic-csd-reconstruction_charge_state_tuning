The U-Nets were given 1.57–8.89 % of the grid (4 × 40 to 24 × 40 rays × points). On the full CSD the same line fit recovers M12 and M21 with a median relative error of 0.53 % and 0.36 % (41/50 and 44/50 devices within 0.005 of the true value): the floor of the fit itself on 100 × 100 px maps. From the U-Net maps the median relative error of M12 falls from 29.3 % at 1.57 % coverage to 10.4 % at 8.89 %, and that of M21 from 28.5 % to 10.4 %; at 8 × 40 (3.11 %) it is 15.8 % and 12.9 %. The U-Net error is not uniform: at 8 × 40 the devices with a true coupling above 0.4 are underestimated (median -46 % for M12, n = 11; -36 % for M21, n = 10), while those below 0.4 are nearly unbiased (+2.3 % and +0.5 %). The interdot slope, and so rho, is not recovered: interdot segments are a few pixels long, the full CSD yields an interdot family on only 8/50 devices (median |dθ_int| 28.3°, median |rho error| 40 %), and the 12–39 devices per U-Net on which an interdot family is found have median |dθ_int| of 20–38°, i.e. spurious pieces. rho is therefore reported but not claimed. A dot family was missing on 0/50 devices for the full CSD and on at most 0/50 for any U-Net.
